Good Questions

Fence & Deck FAQs.

How much does a new fence or deck cost?+
Every yard is different, so we give you a firm written quote after an on-site visit. Pricing depends on material (wood, vinyl, composite, aluminum, or chain-link), linear footage or square footage, and site conditions. We'll walk you through the options at every price point — no pressure.
Are you licensed and insured?+
Ask us and we'll send over our current licence and insurance details, and we pull the proper permits when your project requires them.
Do you offer financing?+
We do. Qualifying projects can be broken into flexible monthly payments so you can build the deck or fence you want now and pay over time. Ask about current plans when we come out for your estimate.
How long does a project take?+
Most residential fences go up in one to three days, and most decks in one to two weeks, depending on size, material, and weather. We give you a realistic timeline in writing before we start and keep you updated with photos along the way.
What warranty do you provide?+
Ask about our material and workmanship warranty before we break ground. If something isn't right, we make it right — that's the whole point of hiring a licensed local builder instead of a weekend crew.
